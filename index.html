Before You Buy

Pre-purchase
inspections

Before money changes hands, the vehicle goes on the lift and gets looked at properly.

  1. On the lift

    Underbody, suspension, exhaust and leaks assessed from below.

  2. Mechanical

    Engine, transmission, brakes, steering and tires checked and measured.

  3. Electronic

    Stored fault codes and system readiness read from the vehicle's modules.

  4. Reported

    Findings written up, so the price you negotiate reflects the car in front of you.
