About the shop

A shop that finds out before it replaces

360 Auto Care is an independent repair shop on North Prospect Avenue in Lynbrook, working on foreign and domestic vehicles — the commuters, family cars, work vans and pickups people actually depend on.

How the work is decided

Nothing gets replaced to find out whether it was the problem

A fault is identified on equipment before a part is ordered. It is a slower way to start a job and a faster way to finish one: what the diagnosis finds is what gets replaced, and nothing else does.

What the work covers

Routine maintenance through to major repair — diagnostics, brakes, tires and alignment, climate, state inspection, detailing and pre-purchase checks.

What comes through the bays

Imported and domestic cars, crossovers, vans and light trucks.

Who the shop is for

An independent shop, in one town

Not a dealership service department and not a chain — one shop on North Prospect Avenue, working on the vehicles that are parked around it the rest of the week.

The area

Lynbrook, Valley Stream, Malverne, Rockville Centre and East Rockaway.

The vehicles

Commuters, family cars, work vans and pickups — imported and domestic, on the same equipment.
